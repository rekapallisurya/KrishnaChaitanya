# Actuarial Assumptions

## Portfolio assumptions

- Portfolio reflects General Liability business with heterogeneous industry risk.
- Exposure is represented at policy-year level and ranges from partial to full exposure.
- Prior claims and latent risk factor increase expected frequency.

## Frequency assumptions

- Claim counts are generated from a Poisson process.
- Mean frequency depends on:
  - exposure
  - industry risk factor
  - prior claims
  - latent risk factor

## Severity assumptions

- Ultimate loss follows a heavy-tailed mixture:
  - 70% small losses
  - 25% medium losses
  - 5% large losses
- Deductibles reduce paid amount subject to a minimum claim floor.
- Coverage limits cap individual claim severity.

## Development assumptions

- Development ages: 12, 24, 36, 48 months.
- Cumulative paid pattern targets 40%, 65%, 85%, 100% by development age.
- Small random noise is applied to development percentages.
